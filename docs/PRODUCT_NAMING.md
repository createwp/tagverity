# Product Naming Direction

**Document type:** Naming recommendation
**Last updated:** 2026-07-29
**Status:** Selected customer-facing working name; clearance pending

## Recommendation

Use **TagVerity** as the customer-facing working product name.

Suggested presentation:

```text
TagVerity
Measurement operations for marketers
```

Suggested product promise:

> From measurement requirement to verified result.

## Why It Fits

### Tag

`Tag` keeps the product connected to a problem marketers and agencies already
recognize. It covers client-side tags, server-side tags, analytics events,
triggers, variables, consent configuration, and destination setup without tying
the brand exclusively to Google Tag Manager.

### Verity

`Verity` means truth. It supports the product's most important differentiator:
the platform does not only create or inspect a tag; it proves what was expected,
what was deployed, what happened in the browser, what reached analytics, and
where the chain broke.

### Product Expansion

The name remains credible for:

- Auditing and data-quality diagnosis
- Measurement contracts and blueprints
- GTM inventory and controlled deployment
- Browser and cross-domain verification
- GA4 and future destination verification
- Monitoring and drift detection
- Developer handoffs and repair workflows
- Natural-language operation through MCP
- Future server-side tagging

## Naming Criteria

The final name should:

- Be easy to say, spell, and remember
- Be understandable to marketers without sounding childish
- Communicate trust, verification, or operational control
- Work beyond GTM and GA4
- Support both agency and standalone customer positioning
- Work as a product brand rather than only a feature description
- Avoid implying autonomous publication or guaranteed data accuracy
- Leave room for a family of clearly named capabilities

## Proposed Product Language

### Category

**Measurement operations platform**

This is the accurate long-term category. `Tagging Partner` remains useful as the
customer-facing relationship and onboarding promise.

### One-Line Description

> TagVerity helps marketers design, deploy, verify, and continuously improve
> website and product analytics measurement.

### Short Tagline Options

1. **From requirement to verified result.**
2. **Know your measurement works.**
3. **Design it. Deploy it. Prove it.**
4. **Tracking you can trust.**

The first option maps most closely to the product strategy and avoids reducing
the platform to monitoring alone.

## Product Capability Names

Use clear descriptive labels inside the product rather than inventing a brand
name for every feature:

- **Audit** - configuration and data-quality assessment
- **Blueprints** - measurement contracts and implementation designs
- **Inventory** - customers, domains, containers, tags, triggers, and variables
- **Deployments** - GTM workspace drafts, diffs, versions, and approvals
- **Verification** - browser, network, GA4, and cross-domain evidence
- **Monitoring** - drift detection, alerts, assignment, and retesting
- **Developer handoff** - implementation and repair documentation
- **Assistant** - natural-language planning and controlled operations

## Alternatives Considered

### MeasureLoop

Strongly represents the closed measurement lifecycle and is broader than tags.
However, an existing radiology product uses `Measureloop`, creating unnecessary
brand and search ambiguity.

### MeasureVerity

Broad and trust-oriented, with no obvious direct measurement-platform collision
in the preliminary search. It is less natural to pronounce and less immediately
connected to tagging than `TagVerity`.

### MetricVerity

Communicates trustworthy metrics but sounds more like a reporting or dashboard
product. The platform operates before metrics exist, including implementation,
deployment, and browser verification.

### MeasureOps

Accurately describes the category but sounds technical for the primary marketer
audience. The term is also already used in a current open-source measurement and
MCP ecosystem.

### SignalProof

Strongly communicates evidence and verification, but it is already used by a
GA4 and GTM auditing specialist as well as businesses in other categories. It
should not be used.

### GTM Auditor

Keep this as the repository and internal working name during development. It is
too narrow for the customer-facing brand because the planned product also owns
requirements, analytics destinations, browser evidence, monitoring, repair,
cross-domain journeys, and server-side measurement.

## Preliminary Collision Check

The initial web search on 2026-07-29 found no obvious product using the exact
name `TagVerity`. This is not legal clearance and does not establish domain,
company-name, social-handle, app-store, or trademark availability.

Names rejected during the preliminary check include:

- `SignalProof`: existing GA4/GTM specialist and other active brands
- `MeasureGuard`: existing automated GA4 monitoring product
- `MeasureOps`: used by an active measurement/MCP software ecosystem
- `MeasureLoop`: existing product in another software category

## Clearance Before Adoption

Before changing the application name or purchasing brand assets:

1. Search IP Australia for exact and confusingly similar marks in relevant
   software and business-service classes.
2. Search international trademark databases for intended sales markets.
3. Check Australian company and business names.
4. Check `.com`, `.com.au`, and practical alternative domain availability.
5. Check major social handles and software directories.
6. Search close spellings such as `Tag Verity`, `TagVerify`, `TagVeritas`, and
   `VerityTag`.
7. Obtain professional trademark advice before filing or a public launch.

## Naming Decision Gate

Do not rename code namespaces, database names, environment variables, or URLs
yet. Adopt the name publicly only after:

- The name is tested verbally with several marketers
- Domain and trademark clearance is acceptable
- The one-line product description is understood without explanation
